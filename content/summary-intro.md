Welcome! KEEL gathers evidence from free public data to help answer one question: as AI tools spread, might humanity be slipping into [gradual disempowerment](methodology.qmd#what-keel-measures)? It draws primarily on two papers: [Kulveit et al. (2025)](sources.qmd#kulveit2025) describe three kinds of systems that depend on human participation (economies, states, and cultures) and suggest ways to measure whether they are drifting beyond human influence; [Chooi et al. (2026)](sources.qmd#chooi2026) offer a framework and proposed metrics for measuring AI-induced disempowerment. KEEL brings together relevant indicators in one place. Today's indicators can show whether the conditions for gradual disempowerment are present, not whether it is happening. Closing that gap means measuring what no public data yet track, such as how many decisions are handed to AI and whether the shift is becoming hard to reverse. The indicator grid keeps [rows open](methodology.qmd#empty-rows-lock-in-and-oversight) for these measures and will fill them as the data appear; meanwhile, we plan to update and expand the current indicators and welcome your [feedback](about.qmd#report-an-error-or-give-feedback).
