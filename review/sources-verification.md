# Sources verification

Every entry in `references/keel.json` was checked against its original on 2026-11-18 before it was added: the landing page, PDF or DOI record was opened (scripted download with text extraction, or a web fetch where the site blocks scripts) and the fields below were compared. Nothing was entered from memory. Titles are given in Chicago headline-style capitalization; where the original uses sentence case, that is the only change.

Chicago date placement (owner question): Chicago 17th ed. author-date puts the year after the authors and, for web pages, blog posts and other items with a full date, repeats the year with the month and day later in the entry ("2026. ... October 27, 2026."). Source: University of Newcastle Library, "Chicago B: Author-Date Style 17th edition", web resources page (https://libguides.newcastle.edu.au/chicago-b/web): "Repeat the year with the month and day to avoid any confusion", with the example "Germano, William. 2017. 'Futurist Shock.' Lingua Franca (blog). Chronicle of Higher Education. February 15, 2017." The Simmons University guide to 17th-edition author-date shows the same repetition. (The Chicago Manual of Style Online quick guide requires a login.) Applied to every entry whose source gives a month or day: preprints, web pages, blog posts and reports. The official 17th-edition CSL style does not apply this consistently (it prints "arXiv, January 28." without the year for preprints), which is one reason the Sources page is rendered by `indicators.bibliography` rather than by citeproc.
